Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data Minimization

PRM CHEATS operates with strict data privacy. We do not log IP addresses, do not store browsing activity, and do not use advertising trackers.

2. Collected Data

We collect only non-reversible cryptographic machine hashes (SHA-256 HWID) to lock single-seat licenses to your hardware, and order reference identifiers to verify purchases.

3. Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. Local storage is used strictly for functional purposes (such as site preferences).

4. Payments

Payment information is processed through external, secure gateways or cryptocurrency networks. We do not store credit card or banking numbers.

5. Inquiries

For questions or data deletion requests, contact our Discord support.
